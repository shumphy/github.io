The Brand

A German luxury brand

SHUMPHY is a premium German menswear brand born in Essen — a city forged in steel, fire and unbreakable spirit. We design clothing for men who know who they are. Every piece carries meaning. Every garment tells a story.


From the industrial heart of Ruhrgebiet to the fashion capitals of Europe and Asia, SHUMPHY stands for one thing: wearing your identity with pride. Premium fabrics. Considered design. Clothing for every moment of a man's life.
